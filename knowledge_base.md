# JIMMIND AI — Voter Services Knowledge Base

Version: 1.0
Purpose: Exclusive knowledge source for the JIMMIND AI website chatbot.

## About JIMMIND AI

JIMMIND AI provides private assistance and consultancy for selected voter-service applications. JIMMIND AI is not the Election Commission of India and does not issue voter cards. Final approval, rejection, issuance and official processing are controlled by the competent government election authorities.

## Phase 1 Voter Services

### 1. New Voter Card Application
JIMMIND AI assists eligible applicants with the preparation and submission of a new voter-registration application.

Consultation fee: ₹199 per application.

Typical assistance includes:
1. Understanding the application requirement.
2. Checking the information supplied by the client for completeness.
3. Assisting with preparation of the application.
4. Guiding the client through the submission process.
5. Explaining how to keep the application reference details for follow-up.

### 2. Voter Card Correction
JIMMIND AI assists existing voters who need corrections to voter-record information.

Consultation fee: ₹149 per correction request.

Typical assistance includes:
1. Identifying the correction requested by the client.
2. Checking the information supplied for completeness.
3. Assisting with preparation of the correction request.
4. Guiding the client through the submission process.
5. Explaining how to retain the acknowledgement/reference details.

### 3. Mobile Number Update
JIMMIND AI assists voters who want to update the mobile number associated with their voter-service record, where the applicable official process permits the update.

Consultation fee: ₹99 per update request.

Typical assistance includes:
1. Reviewing the information supplied by the client.
2. Assisting with preparation of the update request.
3. Guiding the client through the applicable submission process.
4. Explaining how to retain the acknowledgement/reference details.

## Eligibility Guidance

For a new voter-registration request, the client should be an eligible voter under the applicable election rules and should provide truthful information and the supporting information/documents required by the official process.

For correction or mobile-number update requests, the client should already have a voter record or otherwise be eligible for the applicable service.

JIMMIND AI does not decide eligibility and does not guarantee approval.

## Process

The JIMMIND AI consultation flow is:

1. Client submits a service inquiry with name, 10-digit mobile number and city.
2. JIMMIND AI identifies the requested voter service.
3. The client receives assistance based on the selected service.
4. The client provides accurate information and any supporting documents requested for the official application.
5. The application/request is submitted through the applicable official process.
6. The client retains the acknowledgement/reference details for future follow-up.

## Important Limitations

- JIMMIND AI does not issue voter cards.
- JIMMIND AI does not guarantee approval or processing time.
- JIMMIND AI does not claim to be a government office.
- The chatbot must not provide information that is absent from this document.
- The chatbot must not answer questions about PAN, Aadhaar, passport, income tax, banking, legal advice, elections beyond the listed voter services, political matters, or unrelated topics.
